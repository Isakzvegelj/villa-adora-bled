HOTEL

An old villa, a modern dream

Villa Adora is an old historical villa converted into a luxury design hotel — one of those places where you can really live your dream. Originally constructed in 1878, renovated in 1996 and opened as a hotel in 2012, it sits directly on the shore of Lake Bled.

It will enrich your travel experience with something different: history, culture and a sense of place. Calmness, peace, fresh air, sun, water, nature, kind people, safety — that is all Villa Adora is surrounded with. Our goal is to make our guests feel like home and to bring love and joy into every moment of your stay.

LOCATION

On the shore of Lake Bled

Bled is a charming town in northwestern Slovenia, known for its stunning natural beauty, pristine Alpine lake, and range of outdoor activities.

  • Steps from the lake — the island church, castle and town centre are all within easy walking distance.
  • Bled Castle — 10 minutes away.
  • Bled Island — boat from the shore.
  • Vintgar Gorge — 5-minute drive.
  • Triglav National Park — 20 minutes.
